Questions

Can it place trades for me?

No, and that's deliberate. Prop firms prohibit third-party account management. Risk Guard is read-only: watching, warning and reporting.

Do I have to give you my MT5 password?

No. Connection is read-only through an investor password or a broker API token, and credentials are stored encrypted, server-side only.

Which firms does it support?

Any prop firm on MT5. You enter your firm's specific limits, so the alerts match your actual rules.

What if my firm changes its rules?

You update the numbers in your dashboard, and the alerts follow immediately.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a monitoring tool. Trading is your decision and your risk.
